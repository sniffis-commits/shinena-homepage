Delete My Account

Last updated: November 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your Shinena account and all associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

Email privacy@shinena.app with the subject "Delete my account" and the email address associated with your account.

✉️ Email privacy@shinena.app
What happens next:
  • We verify the request against the email address on your account.
  • Your account, progress, XP, streaks, achievements, and all personal data are permanently removed from our servers.
  • You'll receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What data is deleted

  • Account information (name, email, password hash)
  • Study progress, XP, levels, and streaks
  • Recitation logs and accuracy metrics
  • Achievements and badges
  • Leaderboard entries
  • Any issue reports you submitted

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
