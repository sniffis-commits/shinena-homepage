Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Shinena ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data when you use the Shinena mobile app and website.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Name — your display name used in leaderboards and progress tracking
  • Email address — used for account verification, password resets, and optional notifications
  • Password — stored as a secure hash (we never store your plaintext password)
  • Country — optional, used for country-specific leaderboards

Speech and Audio Data

Shinena includes voice recognition for recitation practice:

  • Audio is processed locally on your device where possible
  • Speech transcripts may be sent to our server to evaluate recitation accuracy and award XP
  • Raw audio recordings are not stored on our servers permanently
  • You can disable voice recognition in the app settings at any time

Usage and Progress Data

  • Study progress, XP, levels, streaks, and achievements
  • Which texts (Mishna, Tanakh, Talmud, Rambam, Shulchan Aruch) you study and when
  • Recitation scores and accuracy metrics
  • App settings (language, theme, difficulty preferences)

Contact Form

If you use the contact form on our website, we collect your name, email address, and message to respond to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To evaluate recitation accuracy and award XP and achievements
  • To display leaderboards and progress tracking
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
  • To improve the app and fix bugs
  • To respond to your contact form submissions

3. Data Storage and Security

  • User accounts are stored in a secure database with encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt — we never store plaintext passwords
  • Progress data is synchronized between your device and our servers
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app

4. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Service providers who help operate the app (hosting, analytics) — only as needed to provide the service
  • When required by law

5. Your Rights

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct — update your name, email, or country
  • Delete — delete your account and all associated data from within the app
  • Opt out — disable voice recognition or email notifications at any time

6. Children's Privacy

Shinena is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. International Users

By using Shinena, you consent to the collection, processing, and storage of your data as described in this policy, which may involve servers located in Israel.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us through the contact form on our website or email privacy@shinena.app.
